## Item: MEL-29-01 — Engine-driven hydraulic pump (one system inoperative)

**Category (fictional)**: B — repair interval 3 calendar days.

**Dispatch condition**: May be dispatched with one of two engine-driven hydraulic
pumps inoperative, provided:

1. The remaining hydraulic system is fully serviceable and its own pump has passed
   an operational test within the fictional preceding 10 flight cycles.
2. The electric backup pump (if fitted) is verified operative.
3. Flight crew procedures for single-hydraulic-system operation are briefed
   (fictional reference: QRH 29-1).

**(O) Operations procedure**: Crew reviews degraded braking/steering performance
